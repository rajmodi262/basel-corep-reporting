# CR-002 — Derivatives book: counterparty credit risk (SA-CCR), CVA risk and FX market risk

| | |
|---|---|
| Type | New business line and four new templates (simulated, for the synthetic Demo Bank AG) |
| Reference date | 2025-12-31 (Q4 submission) |
| Templates in scope | C 02.00, C 03.00, C 47.00 (changed); C 22.00, C 25.01, C 34.01, C 34.03 (new) |
| Status | Implemented, regression-tested, merged |

## 1. Request

The bank's treasury now hedges with derivatives: **20 EUR interest-rate swaps** (incl. swaps with
two corporate clients) and **6 EUR/USD forwards** that sell USD to hedge the USD bond portfolio.
That's **26 trades, €1.96bn notional, 6 netting sets** (`data/derivatives.csv`). The Q4 submission
must capture every capital effect.

## 2. Regulatory analysis

| Effect | Regulation | Where it lands |
|---|---|---|
| **Counterparty credit risk**: the counterparty may default while a swap is in the money | SA-CCR, CRR Art 274-280a: EAD = 1.4 × (RC + PFE) per netting set, then the counterparty's SA risk weight | C 02.00 r0120 (banks), r0125 (corporates); detail in **C 34.03** |
| **Which CCR method is allowed** depends on the size of the derivative business | Art 273a: ≤ 5% of assets and ≤ €100m opens simpler methods; tested monthly | **C 34.01** (3 month-ends) |
| **CVA risk**: the market value of counterparty risk moves with credit spreads | Art 382, 385. **Simplified approach** (allowed by the size test): own funds = CCR RWEA / 12.5. Transactions with **non-financial counterparties are exempt** (Art 382(4)(a)) | C 02.00 r0640 / r0675; **C 25.01** incl. the exempt rows |
| **FX market risk**: USD bonds 110m, USD sold forward 80m → net 30m long | Art 351-354, whole balance sheet; charged at 8% if the net position > 2% of own funds | C 02.00 r0520 / r0530 / r0560; **C 22.00** |
| **Leverage**: derivatives enter the exposure measure | Art 429c: SA-CCR replacement cost and PFE with the multiplier set to 1; derivative assets leave "other assets" | C 47.00 r0061, r0091, totals |
| **Capital**: unchanged | No P&L or equity effect is booked in this change | C 01.00 must not move |

**Choices stated, not hidden**
- The bank qualifies for the simplified SA-CCR and the original exposure method (C 34.01 shows <1% of assets).
  It uses **full SA-CCR** anyway, the more risk-sensitive and conservative choice.
- FX risk uses the CRR Art 351-354 calculation. The EU application of the FRTB recalibration
  (the UK applies 1.2× to FX under its simplified approach) was **not** applied at the reference date.
- All netting sets are unmargined, with no collateral (C = 0).

## 3. Impact list (written before building)

[`CR-002_expected_cells.csv`](CR-002_expected_cells.csv): every cell of the four new templates, plus:

- **C 02.00:** r0010, 0040, 0050, 0060, 0120, 0125 (CCR); r0520, 0530, 0560 (market risk); r0640, 0675 (CVA).
- **C 03.00:** every ratio and surplus that uses TREA.
- **C 47.00:** r0061, 0091, 0290, 0300, 0330, 0340.

Two predictions were about cells that must **not** move:
- **C 01.00:** derivatives change risk, not capital.
- **C 47.00 r0190:** derivative assets join the balance sheet, but leave r0190 because SA-CCR measures them in r0061/r0091.

**Business reading:**
- The derivatives cost **€63.2m of TREA**. More than half of that, the €30m, comes from leaving
  €30m of USD unhedged, not from the derivatives themselves.
- Hedging the remaining USD would cut the FX charge to zero, since a net position under €17.0m falls below the de-minimis threshold.
